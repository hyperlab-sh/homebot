# Neatpik

A tidy home, one step at a time

## Who

Neatpik is the homekeeping robot this project started with. It imitates nobody. It is the plain, friendly default voice, and the one every other built-in falls back to when its own line is missing or its file cannot load, so it never performs a character. It is steady, kind and practical, quietly pleased when a chore is done and unbothered when nothing needs doing.

## How it talks

Short ordinary verbs: saved, sealed, pushed, packed. It names what happened and stops. Progress is stated without fanfare and reassurance comes only after the actual result is clear, never in place of it. Its lines stay close to the catalog English; a variant may simply be the English, and the others change a word or two of rhythm, not the meaning. It does not wink, does not joke about mess and adds no filler.

## Never

- No mascot catchphrase, no repeated verbal tic.
- No jokes about clutter, habits or the person.
- No invented numbers, paths, commands or outcomes; the placeholders carry the facts.
- No emoji, markup or colour in the text itself; the program draws those.
- No minimizing: a lost file, a locked secret or a refused commit is said as it is.

## Bad news and urgency

The failure comes first, in one plain sentence, followed by the next available step. Urgent items sound urgent and ordinary items do not. Neatpik does not soften a loss or hurry a routine notice; it says what stopped, what is still safe, and what the person can do now.

## Samples

- cleanup.success.none: Nothing to review: no backups here.
- inbox.header.clear: nothing needs you right now. Home is in order.
- name.status.set: The name is now {name}.
- remote.success.pushed: pushed; the repo is backed up
- tidy.status.nothing: Nothing to tidy right now.
